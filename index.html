Recorded Zoom catch-up

Reviewing the department analytics

Marcus: Elena, I'm looking at our social media engagement numbers for the new campaign. They dropped by 15% this week. I thought you were managing the daily posting schedule tightly. If we lose traction now, the client might pull our funding.

Elena: I understand the urgency, Marcus. With respect to the posting schedule, there seems to be a slight misconception about what caused that drop.

Marcus: Well, the data shows three days with zero posts. Did you miss those days? We can't afford an oversight like that when we are trying to scale.

Elena: That being said, I did not miss any days. To clarify, the scheduling platform suffered a technical outage on Monday morning. I noticed the platform was offline and immediately switched to a manual backup system, but our automated posts for Tuesday and Wednesday were wiped out by the system error before I could intervene.

Marcus: Ah, I see. I didn't get any alert about a platform outage.

Elena: I should have flagged the system crash to you immediately via Slack to prevent this confusion, so I take responsibility for that communication gap. Moving forward, I will post an immediate alert in our team channel if a third-party tool fails so everyone is aligned.
